What's new

Build 8

Oct 29Latest
  • “Try again” and “Rephrase” buttons. When Apple's AI says no or something goes wrong, tap to retry, or put your words back in the box to edit them.
  • New starter ideas. Rewrite this, Summarize, Reply to a text, Make a checklist, Draft an email. These are the things the on-device AI does best.
  • Long chats remember better. When a chat gets long, the app writes a short summary of the earlier part, on your phone, so the AI keeps the thread. A gentle card suggests starting a new chat.
  • Share and Regenerate. Share any answer, or ask for a fresh take on the newest one.
  • Rename and search chats. Swipe right or long-press a chat to rename it. Search finds words anywhere in your chats.
  • Bigger text and VoiceOver. Text follows your iPhone's Text Size setting, and buttons are labeled for VoiceOver.

Build 7

Oct 28
  • Erase everything from the Chats screen. A trash button deletes every conversation, after you confirm.
  • Feedback button. The envelope icon on the Chats screen starts a feedback email.

Known issues

  • 📱
    Needs a newer iPhoneiPhone 15 Pro or newer, on iOS 26, with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri). On older iPhones the app installs but can't chat.
  • ⏳
    First reply can be slowRight after you turn on Apple Intelligence, your iPhone may still be downloading Apple's AI. Give it a few minutes on Wi-Fi.
  • 🔁
    Apple's AI sometimes says noNow and then it turns down an ordinary request. Tap Try again, or Rephrase and say it a little differently.
  • 🛡️
    Some topics are off-limitsApple's on-device AI won't help with explicit or adult content, or some sensitive topics. That's Apple's built-in safety filter, and it can't be turned off. You'll see a note with Try again and Rephrase.
  • 💬
    Very long chats get summarizedThe AI can only hold so much at once. In a very long chat, it works from a summary of the earlier part. A fresh chat gives the sharpest answers.
