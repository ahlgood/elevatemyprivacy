Questions

Does anything leave my phone?

No. The AI runs on your iPhone with Apple's on-device model. Your chats are stored only on your phone, and there's no account, no server and no analytics.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It works in Airplane Mode, which is a simple way to see that nothing is going anywhere.

Is it free?

Free during the TestFlight beta. After launch it will be $1.99 a month.

Which iPhones?

iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, and any iPhone 16 or newer, on iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri).
